This Privacy Policy describes how the Wumek iOS application (“Wumek,” “the app,” “we,” “us”) handles information. Wumek is an on-device HVAC filter log and change timer. It is not a cloud service, not a public social network, and not professional HVAC or medical advice.

By using Wumek you agree to this Policy. The Terms of Service apply as well.

1. Who we are

Wumek is a utility app for logging furnace / HVAC air-filter sizes, timing a Remove → Fit → Run change session, and keeping related notes on the same iPhone. The published display name is Wumek. The App Store marketing version covered by this Policy is 1.0.

We do not operate a Wumek user-content server. Filter cards, photos, voice memos, Trail posts, Care tasks, Season plans, Sift balances, and account flags stay in the app’s sandbox on your device unless you back up the device with Apple tools you control.

2. Information stored on your device

Wumek stores the following locally. It does not require an email address, phone number, or password that you type in.

2.1 Account and session

When you tap Quick Login, Wumek generates a local username (for example a DuctGuest_ name) and a random local password if none exist yet, then sets a session flag. After Log Out, the session flag is cleared and a logout flag is set so a cold start returns to Login; credentials, Sift, and your logs remain on the device until you delete the account. These values live in iOS UserDefaults under the key wumek_db_v1 as part of a single encoded database record.

2.2 Filter logs and tools

The same on-device record may include:

  • Filter Shelf cards (location, size, MERV, cadence, last-changed time, optional notes)
  • Change-session drafts and the last finished session (stage timers, attached photo file name)
  • Care List tasks
  • Season Plan goals and subtasks
  • Duct Notes text
  • My Trail posts, local likes, local comments, and optional Ember Stamp flags
  • Studio tip like/open flags
  • Virtual Sift balance and extra shelf / extra plan unlock counts
  • Whether the first Filter Desk tracking-and-notification prompt chain has already run

2.3 Photos and voice memos

JPEG photographs and AAC voice memos are written to the app Documents folder in a directory named WumekMedia. Records in UserDefaults store file names, not the binary media itself.

Wumek does not use iOS Keychain. Wumek does not embed a third-party analytics SDK.

2.4 Information we do not collect by default

Wumek does not collect your name, email, postal address, payment-card number, precise location, contacts, or health records. Quick Login does not send credentials to us.

Wumek requests system permissions only when a real in-app feature needs them, or on the mandatory first Filter Desk visit described below. The app does not include a permission-management screen, toggles, or “Manage Permissions” settings.

4. Facial data

This Section 4 is written so Apple App Review can cite it under Guideline 2.1 and Guideline 5.1.1.

4.1 What is collected

Wumek does not collect biometric facial data. Wumek does not create faceprints, face geometry maps, Face ID templates, iris scans, or facial-recognition identifiers. Wumek does not run face detection, face matching, liveness checks, or emotion analysis.

Users may capture a new photograph with the camera or attach a photograph from the photo library for filter cards, change sessions, Duct Notes, and My Trail. Those photographs are of furnace filters and related HVAC work. A photograph may incidentally depict a human face if a person stands in the frame, appears in a reflection, or if the user photographs themselves. Any such face is part of an ordinary user-chosen image. It is not a biometric faceprint and is not processed as identity data.

Wumek does not use the TrueDepth camera for face mesh, and it does not use Face ID.

4.2 Complete planned use

Photographs (including any incidental face) are used only so you can see the filter size stamp, seating, or a personal note later inside Wumek. Planned uses:

  • Display on the Filter Shelf card and Filter Editor
  • Display on a change session Fit photo and on a finished-session Trail post if you attach one
  • Display on a Duct Note or a My Trail post you create

Wumek does not use photographs to identify people, authenticate users, unlock the app, moderate other users, train machine-learning models, measure audience demographics, or target advertising.

4.3 Sharing, disclosure, and where stored

Photographs are stored on the device as JPEG files in the app sandbox folder WumekMedia (Documents). File names are stored in the on-device UserDefaults database wumek_db_v1. Wumek does not upload photographs to a Wumek server. Wumek has no user-media backend. We do not sell, rent, or disclose photographs to advertisers, data brokers, or other users. My Trail is a local log on your iPhone; it is not a public feed.

If you use iCloud Backup or another device backup you control, Apple or that backup provider may store a copy of the app sandbox according to their policies. That is not a Wumek disclosure of facial data to a Wumek service.

4.4 Retention

Photographs remain on the device until you delete the related filter, note, or Trail post, replace the photo, or delete your Wumek account. Delete Account removes the WumekMedia folder and the UserDefaults database. Uninstalling Wumek removes the app sandbox, including these files, unless they exist only inside a backup you kept elsewhere.

4.5 This chapter is the explanation

This Section 4 is the complete explanation of Wumek’s collection, use, disclosure, sharing, and retention of facial data, including incidental faces that may appear in user photographs of furnace filters. Wumek does not collect facial data beyond what this chapter describes.

5. Tracking authorization (ATT)

On the first appearance of Filter Desk after login, Wumek calls Apple’s ATTrackingManager.requestTrackingAuthorization. There is no custom “Allow tracking?” screen before the system dialog.

Wumek does not currently integrate a third-party advertising or analytics SDK. The ATT request exists so the system tracking status is determined as required for this app. Wumek does not sell advertising inside the app. We do not use the advertising identifier to build a cross-app profile on a Wumek server, because Wumek has no such server.

6. Push notifications

After the ATT request returns (or if ATT is unavailable on older systems), Wumek calls UNUserNotificationCenter.requestAuthorization for alert, badge, and sound. This happens on the same first Filter Desk visit. There is no separate “Enable Notifications” button elsewhere in the app.

Wumek does not set a UNUserNotificationCenter delegate, does not schedule local notifications, and does not implement remote-notification handlers. The Push Notifications capability is present so the system authorization can be requested. Granting or denying the dialog does not send your filter logs to us.

8. Third-party automated generation services

Wumek does not use a third-party automated generation or classification API.

User data sent to third-party generation APIs: none. Wumek does not send prompts, reference images, voice memos, filter sizes, usernames, Trail text, or any other user content to a third-party generation vendor. Filter logging, timers, and the local Trail run on the device.

StoreKit purchase traffic goes to Apple, not to a generation-service vendor.

9. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell personal information.

We do not share on-device logs with other Wumek users. My Trail, likes, and comments are local.

We may disclose information if required by law, legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of users or others — but Wumek does not hold a cloud copy of your logs that we could produce unless you send it to us yourself (for example by contacting support and attaching a screenshot).

10. Retention

On-device data remains until you delete the related item, delete your account, or delete the app. There is no Wumek cloud retention period because there is no Wumek cloud user database.

11. Account deletion and your choices

Log Out ends the session and keeps credentials, Sift, media, and logs on the device.

Delete Account (Profile) shows a confirmation warning that the action deletes filters, sessions, and Sift on this device. If you confirm, Wumek:

  • Deletes the WumekMedia folder (photos and voice memos)
  • Replaces the UserDefaults database with a fresh empty record (credentials, balances, filters, sessions, tasks, goals, notes, Trail, and flags)

You cannot recover that local data from Wumek after deletion.

You may also delete Wumek in iOS, which removes the sandbox.
